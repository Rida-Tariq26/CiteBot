# Acme Corporation — Contractor & Vendor Engagement Addendum (2026)

## 1. Overview
This Addendum establishes specialized operational guidelines for external consultants, contractors, and agency vendors working under active Statements of Work (SOW) with Acme Corporation.

## 2. Invoicing and Payment Terms
- All contractor invoices are processed on a **Net 30 payment schedule** following verified submission and manager sign-off.
- Invoices must be submitted in PDF format and include SOW reference numbers, project milestones completed, and itemized hour breakdowns.

## 3. Contractor Expense Approvals (Conflict Case)
- Whereas internal staff follow standard company expense thresholds, contractor billable incidentals and travel expenses require prior written approval for amounts exceeding **$75 per single item or meal**.
- Any travel accommodation exceeding $250 per night must be booked directly through Acme Corporate Travel or approved in advance by the VP of Procurement.

## 4. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
- All deliverables, source code, data sets, architectural diagrams, and documentation produced during the contract term constitute work made for hire and remain the exclusive intellectual property of Acme Corporation.
- Access tokens and VPN credentials are provided on a least-privilege basis and are automatically decommissioned upon contract conclusion.
