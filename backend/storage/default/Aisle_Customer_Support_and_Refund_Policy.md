# Acme Cloud Services — Customer Support & Refund Policy

## 1. Scope and Eligibility
This document governs customer return requests, service level commitments, and subscription refund parameters for Acme Cloud Services and Acme Hardware accessories.

## 2. 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
- New customers purchasing annual SaaS subscriptions or standalone software licenses are entitled to a full refund within **30 calendar days of initial purchase**, no questions asked.
- Hardware devices returned in their original packaging with all included cables and accessories qualify for a 100% refund, minus a standard $15 restocking and shipping deduction.
- After 30 days, annual subscriptions may be cancelled at any time to avoid renewal, but past subscription fees are non-refundable.

## 3. SLA & System Uptime Commitments
- Acme commits to delivering **99.9% monthly uptime** for enterprise-tier API and dashboard services.
- If system availability falls below 99.9% in any given calendar month, customers are eligible for service credits:
  - 99.0% – 99.8% uptime: 10% monthly service credit
  - 95.0% – 98.9% uptime: 25% monthly service credit
  - Below 95.0% uptime: 50% monthly service credit
- Credit claims must be submitted to billing@acmecloud.example within 15 days of month-end.

## 4. Hardware Limited Warranty
- All Acme branded IoT hardware and gateway hubs carry a **1-year limited manufacturer warranty** against factory defects in material and workmanship.
- The warranty covers repair, replacement, or re-certified unit dispatch at Acme's sole discretion.
- Accidental water damage, physical impacts, or unauthorized third-party hardware modifications void this warranty.
