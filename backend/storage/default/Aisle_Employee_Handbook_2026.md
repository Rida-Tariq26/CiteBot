# Acme Corporation — Employee Handbook & Workplace Policies (2026 Edition)

## 1. Introduction & Mission
Welcome to Acme Corporation. This handbook outlines the core workplace policies, benefits, and standards that apply to all full-time and part-time staff across all operating offices and remote locations globally.

## 2. Remote Work & Home Office Equipment Stipend
All full-time team members are eligible to work remotely subject to team lead coordination. To ensure an ergonomic and productive home work environment, Acme Corporation provides an annual **home office stipend of $1,200**. 
- The stipend covers monitors, ergonomic chairs, desks, keyboards, and high-speed internet subsidies.
- Expenses must be claimed through the employee portal using receipt attachments within the designated calendar quarter.
- Upgrades to company-provided laptops (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad P-Series) are managed by IT Operations every 24 months.

## 3. Paid Parental Leave Policy
Acme Corporation is committed to supporting expanding families. 
- All regular full-time employees who have completed at least 90 days of continuous service are eligible for **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**.
- This policy applies equally to birth parents, non-birth parents, adoptive parents, and legal guardians welcoming foster children into their household.
- Parental leave may be taken consecutively or split into two distinct blocks within the child's first 12 months.
- Health, dental, and retirement contribution benefits continue uninterrupted during the leave period.

## 4. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Carryover
- Full-time employees accrue **20 days of paid time off per calendar year**, calculated on a pro-rata basis per semi-monthly pay period.
- A maximum of **5 accrued and unused PTO days may roll over** into the subsequent calendar year. Any unused PTO exceeding 5 days is forfeited on December 31 unless an explicit exception is granted by People Operations.
- Bereavement leave provides up to 5 consecutive paid business days for immediate family members.

## 5. Travel & Business Expense Reimbursement
- All business-related travel, lodging, and operational expenses incurred on behalf of Acme Corporation are eligible for reimbursement.
- Any single discretionary purchase or business meal exceeding **$50 requires prior written approval from your Department Manager**.
- All itemized receipts must be uploaded to the expensing system within 30 days of the date the expense occurred. Expenses submitted beyond 45 days will be rejected.

## 6. Core Collaboration Hours
While Acme supports flexible working hours, all remote and in-office team members are expected to be available for synchronous communication, team standups, and cross-functional meetings during **Core Hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST)**, Monday through Thursday.
